## Round 10 — the eight-seat audit

**The decision:** reopen the tavern on DeepInfra with eight competing
models, one immutable briefing, one identical task: audit the completed
corpus. Every finding verified by the orchestrator against the repository
before anything counted. The founder's mandate (use the cache-discount
seats, let models compete) was priced into the round design: per-seat
discount table fetched live from provider metadata.

**The prices:** 9 calls, $0.0509, 40 findings, 19 verified new. MiMo won
quality-per-dollar (6/6 verified at $0.0012); the expensive flagship (Hy4,
$0.016) matched quality at 13× the cost — reserve it for adjudication.
Ling failed its output contract twice (runaway reasoning, 6k then 12k
exhausted) and was priced out — but its sealed 46k-char reasoning contained
the round's deepest insight: **the gates prove integrity, not soundness.**

**The arguments that mattered:**

- *Three seats independently flagged C3's misreading risk* — "σ
  monotone-inert" invites reading σ as a no-op, while E-Q16-B sealed
  completion ~1/σ. Both sealed facts are true; the scoreboard language
  needed the formation scope. Landed in SPEC, CLAIM-VERDICTS, BOUNDARY-MAP.
- *The E-Q14 "80/80 at 256 polluters" reading is baseline-relative* —
  delivery was at ceiling at zero pollution; what pollution costs is
  provenance. The boundary map's phrasing now says so.
- *The orchestrator found what the seats missed*: chain-registry drift —
  four hand-maintained lists had silently diverged from disk (defect #7).
  Hy4's falsifier predicted the class; the instance was e_q13–e_q16, not
  the E-Q9/11/12 it aimed at. Partial credit receipted.

**The fix:** one registry (`chain_registry.mjs`), five consumers rewired,
strays fail PG1, gate re-sealed at 16-chain scope. Plus the e_q16 erratum
naming its superseded run-1 rows machine-readably, and the MIT license
(the #1 adoption blocker, resolved at root and inside the package).

**The judgment layer, priced:** when the TypeSafe key returned mid-round,
Jev 1.13.0 judged all 40 findings in one batched call per seat (~230 ms).
It flagged ZERO hallucinations — rated Qwen's invented mechanism 4/4
verified-new. **The grounding gap**: a briefing-only judge cannot see the
repository. Law adopted: judges draft, the repo decides. This is Xing's
closed-room critique observed live, at $0.00 per seat.

## Round 11 — the fix audit (devil's advocate)

**The decision:** attack the fixes. Founder-endorsed seats only (Hy4,
DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, MiMo + Seed-2.0-mini briefed as attacker — the r10
learning was that an audit-briefed Seed converges to the mean). Two turns
per seat so the cache discounts actually cash: turn 2 self-critiques turn
1, and its prompt is the full turn-1 conversation.

**The prices:** 8 calls, $0.046, 8/8 contracted JSON. MiMo turn 2: 1,952
cached tokens, $0.000715 — the cache game, materialized. Seed under attack
briefing: 4 sharp findings and an honest weakest-finding admission.

**The consensus finding (all four seats, independently):** PG6's curated
claims table — built hours earlier by a subagent — was already another
hand-maintained list. The defect-#7 class, reintroduced at the soundness
layer, caught by the seats before it could drift.

**The fixes that landed (each verified before landing):**

1. **Seal-time registration**: `appendAndVerify` refuses unregistered
   chains. The R10 law was prose enforced at gate-run time; the seal path
   is the class-kill. Negative control: refusal precedes any write.
2. **Coverage law**: every registered chain carries a curated claim or a
   reasoned exclusion; new experiments FAIL PG6 until curated. First run
   caught e_q10 uncovered and got its claim in the same breath: 20/20
   claims, 16/16 chains.
3. **String-compare sweep closed** (no further lexicographic-ordering
   instances repo-wide), **erratum-reading law** (zero current victims,
   grep-receipted), **adjudication artifact** (repo-verified outcomes
   beside the sealed judgments), **registry self-verification** (its own
   sha pinned and drift-checked), **license-copy survival** (fresh
   extractions carry the grant).

**Priced against the seats:** two findings were false (missing-file checks
exist), one was the TCB problem dressed as critical (registered as honest
residue, not fixed by theater). The verification layer earned its keep both
directions: it confirmed the real attacks and refused the fake ones.

orchestrator before it counted — the same law the tavern seats live under.
