# CREATIVE-PRACTICE — the between-rounds discipline

> ORCH-R23, per the operator's standing instruction: "take breaks for
> creative writing between rounds and teach your subagents to do the
> same." This document is that teaching. It is onboarding material for
> every fleet member and seat that works successive rounds on technical
> material.

## The practice, in one paragraph

Between technical rounds — not during, not instead of — write one
small creative piece grounded in the round's actual material. Ground it
in something real and measured: a verdict, a latency number, a
fail-closed stop, a law the round banked. Then bench it: two seats
read it AS-SAID (a devil's advocate and a line critic), the
orchestrator accepts or declines each note ON THE MERITS (declines are
receipted with reasons), the piece ships, and the receipts are
committed beside the code. The break is real rest *because* it is also
real work with real standards.

## Why it works (the claim, and the receipt)

- **The mind needs a different shape of problem.** Eleven rounds of
  pre-registrations and falsifiers benefit from an interlude of
  metaphor. The fleet's experience: pieces written from sealed
  experiment mechanics (the-fourth-state ← the Ψ(τ) algebra;
  the-worms-turn-on ← the E-Q1 watch log; the-shapes-a-constructor-
  leaves ← the E-Q20 verdict species; the-gatekeeper-never-reads-the-
  letter ← the r22 geo law; a-glossary-of-honest-nulls ← the r23
  ablation) are ALSO the round's best documentation-in-spirit: readers
  who cannot parse the chain JSON can parse the play.
- **The bench is a cheap quality instrument.** ~$0.001 of seat time
  per piece has caught: over-explanation, floating abstraction, filler
  beats, unearned endings. It has also been DECLINED when wrong — the
  r22 standing-still pause survived a cut suggestion because it was
  the thesis. Editorial control never leaves the orchestrator.
- **Forms rotate by law.** poem → short story → field guide → play →
  glossary → … never two of the same form in a row. The form is chosen
  by the material's shape: algebra wants verse, watch logs want
  narration, customs officers want a stage, working vocabulary wants a
  glossary.

## The rules (borrow freely, keep the receipts)

1. **One piece per round, one round per piece.** The break is bounded
   or it is not a break. "Mostly technology" is the operator's ratio;
   the piece is the minority time.
2. **Ground it in the sealed record.** Every number that appears in
   the piece appears in a receipt. Creative license lives in the
   metaphor, never in the facts.
3. **Bench two seats, AS-SAID.** One hostile (what is pretentious,
   what is filler), one line-level (strongest three, weakest three,
   does the ending earn itself). Seal the opinions; they are priced
   rows like any other.
4. **Accept and decline with reasons.** "Accepted: the author's note
   cut (both seats converged)." "Declined on facts: the trajectory
   numbers ARE in the sealed verdict table." A decline without a
   reason is a mood, not an edit.
5. **Ship with receipts.** The piece goes to the writing collection;
   the bench rows and the declines go to the technical repo's
   situations/ directory. The pair of commits is the artifact.
6. **Teach it forward.** A subagent that runs creative rounds
   inherits this document and the standing forms list. New forms are
   welcome; repeats are not.
